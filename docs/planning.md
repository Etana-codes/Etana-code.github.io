# Project Proposal: CARE 4 U
A wellbeing and scheduling app and local support app for unpaid carers, disabled people, and people who struggle with their mental health. The app is meant to help unpaid carers balance caring responsibilities with work, education, relationships and leisure and act as a support finder and a personalised daily activity planner for people who have mental health conditions and are disabled.

## Business Case

### Problem statement
 There are a lot of unpaid carers, disabled people, and people with mental health struggles may face different barriers having to organise their everyday activities while also having to balance work, life, relationships, and leisure. They may also find it hard to find local support services that are related to their needs because information is spread through different organisations and websites. These challenges can make every day planning harder, reduce time for rest and personal social activities, and create barriers to getting access to the support needed. The app will provide a platform that will combine personalised daily activity planning, basic wellbeing tools and a listing of local support services in Bristol and later more parts of the Uk. The selected data taken from the open data Bristol will be used to show information about unpaid care, disability, and health around the Bristol ward, which will help users of the app to learn more about the community needs
### Business benefits
There are several potential benefits for this app which include; Improving the Users wellbeing with the use of personalised planning for balancing out responsibilities between work, education, personal time, and social activities. The app could also help improve time management, helping the users plan their schedule much better, organise tasks, work time, study sessions, assignment due dates, and other commitments. The app would also help users get access to support groups, charity and community organisations helping them meet and bond with other people. The app will also make use of low cost software like HTML, CSS, and Javascript without needing complicated and expensive software to build.

### Options Considered
 Options considered 

There were four options considered when deciding how to develop the Care 4 U app. 

Option 1: Do Nothing and make use of the existing resources. 

This option involves making zero changes and making use of the existing resources, this will have no development cost, and users can keep on using the app's existing planners and websites to access support, although with this option information 

Advantages: 

1. No development or maintenance costs. 

2. Users can keep using familiar applications and resources. 

3. No additional software training is needed. 

Disadvantages: 

1. Information remains spread across multiple platforms. 

2. Users must organise and personalise their own daily activities and routines. 

3. Finding suitable local support services may take too much time. 

 

Option 2: Use Existing Apps 

This option would require the use of existing calendar, scheduling and wellbeing apps to manage the users activities, and track their mental wellbeing. 

Advantages: 

1. Most existing apps may already provide scheduling, reminders, and mental wellbeing tracking features. 

2. Cost less for development compared to when building a new application. 

3. Users can find benefits from established software and existing functionality. 

Disadvantages: 

1. Users may need to switch between different applications.  

2. Information on some local support services may not be accessed easily and may also be incorrect. 

3. Users may find it hard to manage all their tasks and activities across different platforms. 

Option 3: Develop a local support service assigned repository 

This option would involve developing a centered repository where users can easily search for local support services, organisations, and charities. 

 

Advantages: 

1. Makes local support services easier to find and access. 

2. Provides a centralised location for important support information 

3. May help unpaid carers, people with mental health conditions and people with disabilities find assistance. 

 

Disadvantages: 

1. It does not provide the features needed for organising day-to-day activities and tasks. 

2. Users would still require a separate app for scheduling and wellbeing. 

3. Information about local support services would need regular updates to remain correct. 

 

Option 4: Develop C	are 4 U as an all-in-one application 

This option would involve developing a combined application that would bring together daily activity planning, responsibility scheduling, reminders, mood, and wellbeing tracking, local services and inserting the data census using Bristol ward data.  

Advantages: 

1. Provides a lot of useful features in one application. 

2. Reduces the need for users having to switch between multiple applications, 

3. Allows users to organise their daily activities, responsibilities and access local support on one platform. 

4. Provides an easier and more accessible experience for users. 

 

Disadvantages: 

1. Will require a lot of time to build, develop, and may need more resources. 

2. Requires a lot of testing, making sure that all the features work accurately. 

3. Ongoing maintenance will be needed to make sure information about local support services is accurate, and the application is kept useful for users. 

 

Preferred option 

Option 4 has been chosen to be the preferred option because it offers the most extensive solution to all the problems identified. By combining activity planning, wellbeing and mood tracking and access to various local support services. Care 4 U focuses on making day-to-day organisation and access to getting assistance easier for the users. Although this option would require a lot of development and continuous maintenance, the benefits of having these features in one application would make it a better option. 
### Expected Risks
Expected risks 

There are several risks that have been thought about during the planning and development process of Care 4 U. These risks may affect the application's reliability, security, and accessibility. 

| Risk | Potential impact | Possible mitigation |
|---|---|---|
|Inaccurate or outdated local service information |Users may try to get information from organisations that have closed, moved away, or are no longer providing services. |Regularly review and maintenance of local support listings to keep accurate information. 
|Data protection and privacy issues | Users may give out sensitive information in relation to their health and wellbeing, caring roles which may result in potential privacy issues. |Enable secure authentication, appropriate access controls and clear privacy settings. 
|Technical difficulties | Users may have problems finding on using the application, accessing features or completing activities. | Conduct continuous testing and provide a simple, user friendly window. 
|Data loss | Users may lose essential information like plans, task reminders, or wellbeing records. | Create reliable data storage and reasonable backup procedures. 
|Accessibility barriers  | Users with visual, physical, and cognitive impairments may find it difficult to use some features of the application. | Design the application to with user accessibility, which includes read aloud texts, easy understanding, good colour grading, and assistive technology support. 
|Irrelevant support information | Users may find that local service shown do not meet up with their individual needs or situations. | Provide search and filtering options that will allow users to find relevant services to suit their needs and location.
